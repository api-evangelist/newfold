# Newfold Labs Standards

> How we build software at Newfold Labs on WordPress and WordPress-adjacent
> codebases. Standards are filed by scope: general ones hold everywhere,
> platform ones bind a technology, artifact ones bind a kind of thing we ship,
> and process covers how we work together. Every document has a stable id;
> cite it when answering "how do we do X".

Each entry below is `[title](url): summary`, followed by the document id, the
artifact types it applies to, and whether conformance is machine-checkable.
